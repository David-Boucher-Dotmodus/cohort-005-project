# Cadence

An online course platform where Learners buy courses and work through them.

## Language

### People

**Learner**:
A person who buys and takes courses.
_Avoid_: Student, client, customer

### Refunds

**Refund Request**:
A Learner's ask to have a purchase paid back, which is Requested until it is Approved or Declined.
_Avoid_: Cancellation, reversal, chargeback, refund (for the request itself)

**Refund**:
Money paid back to a Learner as the result of an Approved Refund Request; it ends the Learner's access to the course.
_Avoid_: Cancellation, reversal, chargeback, money back

**Refund Window**:
The 14 calendar days after a purchase, including the fourteenth, during which a Learner may raise a Refund Request.
_Avoid_: Cancel window, cancellation period

**Refund Amount**:
What the Learner actually paid for the purchase; a Refund is always for this amount.
_Avoid_: Refundable amount, list price
